Data coverage

What was filtered?

The rapid-change pupil screen is a conservative heuristic, not validated blink detection. It marks samples for this derived analysis only. The source CSV is untouched. Availability-only and MAD 4/6/8 sensitivity results remain exported.

Missing or low-coverage windows are not imputed. They appear without model assignments. World head/camera discontinuity pairs are screened separately; camera rotation is not necessarily physical head rotation.

    Three reported episodes

    Ratings were supplied by the player; stored labels remain draft researcher coding. Different labels' intensity scores are not automatically one common confusion scale. Equal-duration references are unlabelled same-region periods, not confirmed non-confused controls.

    Pupil screening sensitivity

    Unsupervised recorded-feature patterns

    K-means did not use the human labels, scores, participant identity or future self-reports. Neutral pattern IDs describe this session's feature combinations. They do not mean Confusion, Stuck, engagement or insight.

    Whole Study model

    Hub-only model

    Fitted separately within Hub. Its P01/P02 IDs are local to this model and cannot be equated with the whole-Study IDs.

    Within-region change point

    Offline retrospective segmentation uses the entire sequence, including behavioral verdicts in the five-second window. A boundary near a successful action is not an early prediction of insight or a precise psychological onset. A 20-second minimum segment also cannot resolve a six-second Confusion episode.

    Pupil around insertion-status events

    Only normalized target-status records with the legacy “Filled” insertion identifier are counted; raw/normalized duplicates and parent completion cascades are not added together. Baseline: −2 to −0.5 s. Post window: +0.5 to +3 s. No pupil interpolation.

    Correct events cluster near the end of the task. The all-event curves have overlapping neighborhoods. There are zero eligible isolated correct events, so this recording does not establish an independent correct-versus-wrong pupil effect. The displayed inter-event IQR is not a confidence interval.

    Observed contact transitions

    Top transitions between consecutive valid callback contacts, same correction context and ≤0.1 s apart. No transition is invented across an invalid, empty or unobserved contact. Blue object nodes and yellow surface nodes retain different provenance.

    Methods and exports

    Interpretation boundaries

    • Tutorial before Pre-room is excluded from these analyses but remains in the original archive.
    • Mapping-verified callbacks: 0. Object identities are traceable, but measured spatial precision was not established in P027.
    • Eye aligned time, Unity callback time and video launch estimates are different time sources. This report does not measure end-to-end synchronization error.
    • Regional pupil centering is not a neutral baseline. Retinal luminance was not measured; visual context can change pupil size. Luminance/pupil study.
    • The rapid-change screen is not validated blink removal. Compare raw and screened estimates and retain exclusions. Pupil preprocessing study.
    • Adjacent windows and sensor rows are not independent participants or trials. No population significance test, supervised accuracy or cross-participant generalization is claimed.
    • Audio/video remain review material. No speech transcription or inference from voice was performed here.
